# 🛒 Retail Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing **retail sales performance** across different categories, segments, states, and time periods. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of key sales metrics, top-performing customers, and regional insights.

## 📊 Overview

This project focuses on creating a **Retail Sales Dashboard** to track and visualize sales across various dimensions such as **product category**, **customer segment**, **region**, and **time**. It enables stakeholders to make informed decisions by providing real-time insights into business performance.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- To clean, transform, and visualize retail sales data.
- To identify top-performing categories, customers, and time periods.
- To enable dynamic filtering based on state and segment.
- To build an executive-ready dashboard that is interactive and insightful.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Data Visualization**: Power BI
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation**: Power Query
- **Data Source**: Retail Sales CSV / Excel File
- **Visualization Elements**:
  - Bar charts
  - Line graphs
  - Pie chart
  - Map visualization
  - Slicers (State Filter)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Sales by Category & Sub-Category**: Visual comparison of performance across product lines.
- **Sales by Segment**: Pie chart distribution among Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office.
- **Monthly Trends**: Line chart depicting seasonality and monthly fluctuations.
- **Top 10 Customers**: Ranked bar chart of highest revenue-generating customers.
- **Sales by State (Map)**: Regional sales distribution using bubble map.
- **Dynamic Filters**: Interactivity through state-wise selection.

## 📌 Insights & Analysis

- **Phones** and **Chairs** are the top-selling sub-categories.
- The **Consumer** segment accounts for the highest share of revenue (~50%).
- **Sean Miller** is the top customer with sales exceeding **25K**.
- **Sales peak in November and December**, indicating a seasonal pattern.
- High sales concentration in cities like **New York**, **Toronto**, and **Chicago**.
